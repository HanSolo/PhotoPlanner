## PhotoPlanner

A little swift app that can help to plan for example landscape or architectural shots. 
You can setup the camera(s) and lens(es) that you use and place the camera on the map and
select the motif on the map. The app will show you the field of view and the depth of field
for the selected combination of camera, lens, aperture and focal length.
In addition you can also overlay the sunrise, sunset, blue- and golden-hours for a selected
date and time. 

Please find an overview here https://github.com/HanSolo/PhotoPlanner/wiki
